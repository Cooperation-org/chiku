title: "Pie-slicing value on stories, and full sprints"
---

Stories now carry their pie-slicing worth, and Kanban grows into Scrum:
plan backlog work into sprints, work the current sprint on the board, then
close it — unfinished stories roll over to the next sprint.

### Added

- **Story value (team + cash)** — every story shows a value badge (`50 cook`,
  `100 usd`, matching the `mcp-taiga` tag convention); unvalued stories show
  an amber "No value" dot. Click the badge in the story view to set both
  numbers — they land on the story's tags, so GovKit's next sync picks them
  up with no backend change. Cash is required at the form level (prefilled
  `0`; the backend treats missing cash as 0 and it never blocks a drop).
- **Value column in the backlog** — team value and cash at a glance, next to
  points. Note: `usd` cash tags are stored correctly today but not yet
  counted by GovKit's pie (it reads cash from a custom field); team `cook`
  value flows end to end now.
- **Sprints view** — create sprints with a start/finish window, track points
  progress per sprint, jump to the sprint's board, close and reopen.
- **Backlog planning** — the backlog now groups unassigned stories plus one
  section per open sprint, each with a sprint picker to plan or unplan a
  story in place. Stories also carry a sprint picker in the story view.
- **Sprint board scope** — open any sprint's board via `?sprint=<id>`; moves
  still PATCH status with optimistic updates.
- **Close with rollover** — closing lists every unfinished story and moves
  them to the next open sprint (or back to the backlog) before the sprint
  closes, so nothing strands in a closed sprint.
- **Deadline countdown** — sprints show a badge that turns amber within 24
  hours of the finish date and red past it, always naming where unfinished
  work will move.

### Changed

- Units default to `cook` / `usd` unless the deployment sets `VITE_VALUE_UNIT`
  / `VITE_CASH_UNIT`.
- All date fields use the shadcn calendar picker (single dates for due dates
  and filters, a range picker for sprint dates) instead of native date inputs.
- Sprint assignment uses one shared `TaskSprintSelector` (shadcn Select) in
  the backlog, the story view, and the close dialog — unassigned reads
  "None".
