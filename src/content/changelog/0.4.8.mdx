New here? Chiku now greets first-time visitors with a short tour: where
work becomes ownership, how story value works, and how sprints close —
instead of dropping you on a board with no context.

### Added

- **Welcome dialog** — the first visit shows a three-step tour (work →
  value → ownership) with your deployment's own branding. It opens once per
  browser and never hijacks story, comment, or sprint deep links.
- **`?onboarding=preview`** — append it to any page to reopen the tour on
  demand, handy for screenshots and demos. Preview mode never touches the
  seen flag.
